## 8. Investigation-Derived Constraints (Papyro UX)

- Ad and interruption policy: avoid intrusive ads, pop-ups, and noisy overlays.
- Mobile-first reading: design for users consuming content in transit on phones.
- Reader behavior reality: optimize for passive reading first, comments second.
- Content preference: prioritize deep niche content discoverability.
- Subscription trust: avoid spam-like flows; make frequency and value explicit.
- Clarity over decoration: simple structure must beat ornamental complexity.

## 9. UX Acceptance Criteria

- Clarity: users can explain page purpose in under 5 seconds.
- Focus: one dominant primary action per screen context.
- Readability: scanning works without visual clutter.
- Feedback: every async or risky action has visible system feedback.
- Recovery: all error states provide a clear next action.
